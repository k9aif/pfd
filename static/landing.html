How much is enough, and when do you get there?

Your freedom number, the years until you reach it, and the investment risk that gets you there, worked out from your own numbers. Nothing leaves this machine.

Your real FIRE number

Retirement spend after tax, divided by a withdrawal rate suited to a long retirement, plus money set aside for healthcare before 65 and the years before Social Security. Home equity stays out.

Advice tested on you

Low, medium and high risk portfolios, each run through 2,000 simulated markets with your savings and spending. You see the chance your money lasts, not a single hopeful line.

Stress tests

A crash the year you retire, your company stock halving, a sabbatical, a lower withdrawal rate. Each shows how far your date moves.

How it works

  1. Enter rounded totals. Categories, not accounts: taxable, retirement, cash, the house, company stock.
  2. Set your "enough". What your chosen life costs per year, not what you spend today.
  3. Press Advise. See which risk level gets you there with the most margin, and what to fix first.
  4. Update once a month. Log the month, watch net worth and market growth, export a copy.

What it is

  • A planning tool for up to three people, with a shared household view
  • Every formula shown in plain English with your numbers
  • Strategy-level guidance: asset mix, cash reserves, concentration, tax access

What it is not

  • Connected to your accounts. It cannot see or move money
  • A stock picker. It never names funds or tickers
  • Financial, tax or legal advice. Take the numbers to a licensed advisor

Sign in

No one can sign in yet. Whoever looks after this computer adds people (up to three) from a terminal:

./run.sh adduser yourname

Then restart the dashboard. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes in the .env file.
